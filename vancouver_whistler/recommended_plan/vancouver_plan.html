Twelve Days · British Columbia · Easy Pace

Vancouver & Whistler.

A laid-back family summer on the coast and in the mountains — built around one focused thing most days, with plenty of room to do nothing. The Vancouver half is North-Shore forest, ocean, and city sights; the Whistler half is alpine scenery, culture, and a little adventure for those who want it. Tracks split where the group does: galleries, makers, and gentle outings for Becky and Kit; hiking and animals for Orlando, ropes and climbing for him and Eli; escape rooms for everyone who's in.

Vancouver — the North Shore week

Deep Cove base, your own SUV from day one. Lean into the North Shore; dip into the city on the days you feel like the trip.

Day 1
Tue, Jul 7 · Arrive Deep Cove · land, settle, eat

Wheels down at YVR around 7:55pm. Pick up the RAV4 and drive across to Deep Cove in North Vancouver (~30–40 min depending on the bridge; allow time to clear customs and the rental counter first). Check into the Vrbo, drop bags, and walk into the tiny village for a late dinner — Deep Cove is a quiet seaside pocket, not a city, which is exactly the right first night. Nothing scheduled. Sleep.

Day 2
Wed, Jul 8 · Capilano · ease in, close to home easy / all

The gentlest possible start, and it's on your side of the water: Capilano Suspension Bridge Park — the big swaying bridge over the canyon, plus the Treetops walkways and the Cliffwalk. It's all walking, no exertion, and genuinely impressive. Buy a timed-entry ticket ahead (peak-summer midday windows fill); if you'd rather a slower, cheaper evening, the park runs a 25%-off Twilight rate after 5pm in summer. If you'd prefer free and even lower-key, Lynn Canyon Park has its own (free) suspension bridge and forest trails nearby. Back to Deep Cove for dinner.

Day 3
Thu, Jul 9 · Grouse Mountain · one mountain, two speeds active easy / all

A rare activity where the group splits without splitting up: ride the Skyride gondola up Grouse Mountain (North Shore, reachable without going downtown). At the top, Eli & Orlando hit the Gravity Coaster and the Ziplines (book both ahead — coaster ride-times and zipline slots fill in summer). Everyone else stays with the gondola crowd: the grizzly-bear Wildlife Refuge, the Lumberjack Show, Birds in Motion — all included with mountain admission, no exertion required. Everyone meets up top for lunch and the view. Quiet evening back in the Cove.

Day 4
Fri, Jul 10 · Granville Island · make something arts

First proper city day. Head downtown — park once (the island's own lots are tight) and hop the little Aquabus across False Creek to Granville Island — the Public Market (free, no booking) is a long, happy graze. The focused thing, a Becky-and-Kit one: a glassblowing class at Vancouver Studio Glass right on the island (book ahead; single-session, make a paperweight), while Orlando and Eli wander the market and the boatyard. Ferry back, easy night.

Day 5
Sat, Jul 11 · Escape room + a show · your people, your puzzles arts easy / all

Daytime: the thing you and Kit actually came for — an escape room. Take your pick — Find & Seek in the city, or Trapped in Burnaby (an easy drive now you've got the car) — or, for something the whole group can do on foot, Vancouver Mysteries is a self-guided outdoor mystery game through Gastown with a costumed "detective." Evening: catch Come From Away — the Tony-nominated Canadian musical about the Newfoundland town that took in 7,000 air passengers stranded after 9/11 (Arts Club's acclaimed production at the Stanley on South Granville; Sat Jul 11 has a 7:30pm). It's the musical you actually want — not the Stanley-Park kids' titles — and a fitting story for a Canada trip. (Bard on the Beach Shakespeare is the alternative if you'd rather a play.) Book ahead.

Day 6
Sun, Jul 12 · Deep Cove day · do less easy / all

A genuine nothing day in the Cove — this is the "take it easy" the trip is built around; anyone who'd rather just hang at the house, hang. For the restless: the easy Quarry Rock walk leaves right from the village for a payoff view (verify it's open — the trail has had on-and-off closures; check the District of North Vancouver notices the week of), a good one for the hikers; or Deep Cove Kayak rents boats and SUPs steps from the door (skip the paddle if an arm's not up for it). Ice cream, water, sunset.

Day 7
Mon, Jul 13 · Last Vancouver day · a cultural cap arts

Easy, walkable morning: a Forbidden Vancouver walking tour (Lost Souls of Gastown, or Dark Secrets of Stanley Park — ~2 hrs, cultural, low-key, book ahead) is a nice cap on the city. Spend the rest of the day at the Cove's pace, and pack up tonight — tomorrow you move to the mountains.

Whistler — the mountain stretch

You have the car now. Four nights, an easy rhythm, and the alpine doing most of the work.

Day 8
Tue, Jul 14 · Drive up + Vallea Lumina · arrive, then glow easy / all

Check out of Deep Cove by 11am and drive the Sea-to-Sky Highway (Hwy 99) north — one of the great coastal drives, ~2–2.5 hrs to Whistler. Make one stop, not three: either the Sea to Sky Gondola in Squamish (Sky Pilot suspension bridge and viewing decks, walk-up, ~$74 — plus easy trails up top, a good first hike for Orlando; the strenuous free Stawamus Chief is right nearby if he wants a proper climb) or Britannia Mine Museum (underground train, the big "BOOM!" show — good for Eli). Shannon Falls is a free 5-minute roadside stop right by the gondola. Into the Whistler townhome at 4pm; dinner in the pedestrian Village. Then the easy showpiece — tonight at 9:15pm: Vallea Lumina, a glowing 1.5-km night walk through the forest with light and sound. Gentle pace (a few sets of stairs), great for everyone, and mid-July dusk lands right at showtime. Booked — 9:15pm, roundtrip shuttle included (shuttle departs at showtime), so you don't even drive to it.

Day 9
Wed, Jul 15 · Bear viewing · gentle, whole group easy / all

A gentle whole-group day: a bear-viewing tour — small-group, 4×4, you watch black bears on the ski slopes from the vehicle. Sedentary, scenic, and genuinely everyone's speed (book ahead; sunrise/sunset slots go first) — Orlando's animal pick, and an easy win for the whole group. Bear viewing is short, so it leaves Orlando an open afternoon for an easy Whistler hike — the Lost Lake loop or the Train Wreck suspension-bridge trail, both flat, forested, and free. Keep the evening loose after last night's late walk — Village dinner, early night.

Day 10
Thu, Jul 16 · Split day · air for two, arts for two active arts

Eli & Orlando get the big one — the thing Eli asked for: The Adventure Group's Aerial Obstacle Course (a real treetop ropes course) at Cougar Mountain, 10 min north of the Village; the Day Ticket opens up the rest of their basecamp too. Heads-up: under-16s need an adult on the tour, so you're on it with him — which is the point, it's your lane too. (If you'd both rather pure ziplines, Ziptrek in the village is the alternative.) Becky & Kit take the arts track, both walk-up and steps apart in the Village: the Audain Art Museum (superb BC art collection — open Thu, ~$22) and the Squamish Líl̓wat Cultural Centre (First Nations culture, hourly guided tours, gentle 3pm forest walk). Regroup for dinner. Low-key evening — Friday's an early start.

Day 11
Fri, Jul 17 · Via Ferrata + Peak 2 Peak · the booked one active easy / all

Via Ferrata, 9:00 AM — booked (Orlando + Eli), up in the alpine via the Whistler lift. Meanwhile Becky & Kit ride the PEAK 2 PEAK gondola up — the scenic centerpiece of the whole trip: glass-bottom cabins between Whistler and Blackcomb, the Cloudraker Skybridge and Raven's Eye viewpoints, no exertion. Everyone meets at the top for lunch with the climbers when they finish. Ask the Via Ferrata operator whether sightseeing lift access is already bundled before buying separate Peak 2 Peak tickets. Keep the afternoon loose — it's a half-day of effort for the climbers.

Day 12
Sat, Jul 18 · Drive back + fly home · unwind the road

Check out by 10am. Drive back down the Sea-to-Sky to YVR — leave Whistler by about 12:30–1pm to clear the ~2.5-hr drive, car return, and check-in comfortably for the 5:50pm flight. (If you're hungry for one last stop and time allows, Squamish has good lunch.) Home to San Diego by ~9pm.

Practical

Getting around

You've got the RAV4 from arrival, so the North Shore (Capilano, Grouse, Lynn Canyon, Deep Cove) and the Sea-to-Sky drive are all easy. Downtown, the catch is parking, not access — park once (or take transit in) and walk/Aquabus around Granville Island and Stanley Park, where lots are tight and pricey.

Driving the Sea-to-Sky

Hwy 99 from Vancouver to Whistler is ~120 km / 2–2.5 hrs, paved and spectacular but winding with few passing lanes. Don't over-plan stops — one is plenty. Fuel up before you leave the city. The same drive back on Jul 18 needs a real time buffer before a 5:50pm flight.

Taking it easy — the skip list

For anyone sitting out the high-exertion stuff: skip ziplines, ropes/Tree adventures, Via Ferrata, whitewater rafting, and kayak/SUP/canoe; glassblowing has some arm work, so it's optional. Everything tagged easy / all is built to be comfortable for everyone.

Eli on his own (15)

Whistler Village is car-free and self-contained — Eli can roam it solo: food, the arcade, shops, the skate/bike-park base, the lake. The harnessed adventures (ropes course, ziplines, Via Ferrata) all require an adult for under-16, so those run with Orlando — no hardship, they're his lane too.

Money & weather

Prices here are Canadian dollars; tap-to-pay is universal. Mid-July is peak summer — warm and dry in town, cooler and breezy in the alpine (bring a layer for Peak 2 Peak and the evening shows). Long daylight, ~9:15pm sunset.

Hours that bite

Audain Art Museum is closed Tue/Wed (do it Thu Jul 16). SLCC is closed Mon. Whistler Sliding Centre self-tour runs Wed–Sun. Capilano timed windows and Vallea Lumina showtimes are fixed — book the slot you actually want.

A couple of corrections

"Bridgetown Forge" is in Portland, Oregon — not Vancouver; use Fraser River Forge if you want blacksmithing (arm-heavy, Eli/Orlando only). "Par-Tee Putt" is a downtown mini-golf bar (670 Smithe St), not on Granville Island. "Time Escape" / "i-Exit" couldn't be verified — stick to the named rooms above.
