Nobody's locked in — hand the group the menu and let everyone pick per day. A few things are whole-group or already booked.

The two lanes

A menu, not an assignment. Same trip, two speeds — choose fresh each day.

  • Fast  The active option — hiking, climbing, ropes, ziplines, coasters, kayaking, escape-room sprints.
  • Slow  The gentle option — scenic, cultural, galleries, makers, markets, easy walks, sitting by the water.
  • All  Whole-group or already booked — the night shows, the drive days, Vallea Lumina, Via Ferrata.

Nobody's pre-slotted. Anyone can fast-lane one day and slow-lane the next. A note on the high-exertion fast-lane stuff (ropes, ziplines, paddling, rafting): not everyone will want it, and there's always a slow-lane alternative the same day.

Vancouver — the North Shore week

Deep Cove base, your own SUV from day one. Lean into the North Shore; dip into the city when you feel like it.

Day 1
Tue, Jul 7 · Arrive Deep Cove · land, settle, eat
AllWheels down at YVR around 7:55pm — pick up the RAV4 and drive to Deep Cove in North Vancouver (~30–40 min; allow time for customs and the rental counter). Drop bags, walk into the tiny seaside village for a late dinner. Nothing scheduled. Sleep.
Day 2
Wed, Jul 8 · North Shore
FastGrouse Mountain — Skyride gondola up, then the Gravity Coaster and the Ziplines (book ride times ahead; slots fill in summer).
SlowCapilano Suspension Bridge — the big swaying canyon bridge, Treetops walkways, Cliffwalk; all easy walking (timed entry — book; 25% off after 5pm). Or the free Lynn Canyon bridge nearby. Grouse's gondola + grizzly Wildlife Refuge is a gentle option too, same mountain.
Day 3
Thu, Jul 9 · Deep Cove & the water
FastDeep Cove Kayak / SUP on the inlet (rentals steps from the Vrbo), then the Quarry Rock walk for the lookout — verify the trail's open; it's had on-and-off closures.
SlowPotter around Deep Cove village — coffee, the famous Honey's doughnuts, browse the shops, sit on the waterfront. A real do-nothing morning.
Day 4
Fri, Jul 10 · Downtown & Granville Island
FastRent bikes and ride the Stanley Park Seawall loop (flat, ~10 km, ocean the whole way) — or hit a downtown escape room.
SlowGranville Island via the little Aquabus — the Public Market graze, plus an optional glassblowing class at Vancouver Studio Glass (book ahead).
Day 5
Sat, Jul 11 · Puzzles + a show
FastAn escape room — Find & Seek in the city, or Trapped in Burnaby (easy drive). For something whole-group on foot, Vancouver Mysteries is a self-guided Gastown game.
SlowA wander through Gastown or the seawall, easy lunch, save energy for tonight.
AllEvening: Come From Away (7:30pm) — the Tony-nominated Canadian musical about Gander, Newfoundland taking in 7,000 stranded passengers after 9/11. Book ahead.
Day 6
Sun, Jul 12 · Nothing day · on purpose
AllA genuine day off in the Cove — this is the "take it easy" the trip is built around. Hang at the house, swim, read. For the restless: repeat Quarry Rock or the kayaks, or a beach afternoon. No agenda.
Day 7
Mon, Jul 13 · Last Vancouver day
FastA North Shore hike — the Lynn Canyon loop or Quarry Rock — to bank one more before the mountains.
SlowA Forbidden Vancouver walking tour (Lost Souls of Gastown / Dark Secrets of Stanley Park — ~2 hrs, cultural, book ahead), or a café day. Pack tonight.

Whistler — the mountain stretch

You have the car. Four nights, an easy rhythm, the alpine doing most of the work — and the same two lanes each day.

Day 8
Tue, Jul 14 · Drive up + Vallea Lumina
FastBreak the Sea-to-Sky drive in Squamish: the Sea to Sky Gondola with trails up top, or the strenuous free Stawamus Chief climb.
SlowA gentler drive stop: Shannon Falls (5-min roadside) + the gondola ride for the view, or the Britannia Mine Museum (underground train, the "BOOM!" show).
AllCheck in 4pm; dinner in the Village. 9:15pm — Vallea Lumina (booked): a glowing 1.5-km night walk, roundtrip shuttle included, so you don't even drive to it.
Day 9
Wed, Jul 15 · Whistler your way
FastA Whistler hike — the Train Wreck suspension-bridge trail or alpine trails off the lift — or a day in the Whistler Mountain Bike Park.
SlowA bear-viewing tour — small-group 4×4, watch black bears on the ski slopes from the vehicle (book ahead; gentle, scenic, anyone can join).
Day 10
Thu, Jul 16 · Split day · pick your speed
FastThe Adventure Group's Aerial Obstacle Course (a real treetop ropes course) at Cougar Mountain, 10 min north — or Ziptrek ziplines in the village. Note: under-16s need an adult on the tour, so Eli pairs with an adult on these.
SlowTwo walk-ups, steps apart in the Village: the Audain Art Museum (superb BC art, open Thu, ~$22) and the Squamish Líl̓wat Cultural Centre (First Nations culture, hourly tours, gentle 3pm forest walk).
Day 11
Fri, Jul 17 · Via Ferrata + Peak 2 Peak
FastVia Ferrata, 9:00 AM — booked (Orlando + Eli): a guided cable-and-rung climb in the alpine via the Whistler lift.
SlowPEAK 2 PEAK gondola — glass-bottom cabins between Whistler and Blackcomb, the Cloudraker Skybridge and Raven's Eye viewpoints, no exertion. Ride up and meet the climbers at the top for lunch.
Day 12
Sat, Jul 18 · Drive back + fly home
AllCheck out by 10am. Drive the Sea-to-Sky back to YVR — leave Whistler by about 12:30–1pm to clear the ~2.5-hr drive, car return, and check-in for the 5:50pm flight. Home to San Diego by ~9pm.

Practical

Getting around

You've got the RAV4 from arrival, so the North Shore (Capilano, Grouse, Lynn Canyon, Deep Cove) and the Sea-to-Sky drive are all easy. Downtown, the catch is parking, not access — park once (or take transit in) and walk/Aquabus around Granville Island and Stanley Park, where lots are tight and pricey.

Driving the Sea-to-Sky

Hwy 99 to Whistler is ~120 km / 2–2.5 hrs, paved and spectacular but winding with few passing lanes. One stop is plenty. Fuel up before you leave the city. The drive back on Jul 18 needs a real buffer before the 5:50pm flight.

The slow lane is a real answer

Not everyone wants ropes, ziplines, paddling, or rafting — and that's the design. Every day has a gentle, scenic, or cultural option, and "just the house" is always on the menu. Pick per person; regroup for dinner.

Eli (15) solo time

Whistler Village is car-free and self-contained — Eli can roam it on his own: food, the arcade, shops, the bike-park base, the lake. The harnessed adventures (ropes course, ziplines, Via Ferrata) all require an adult for under-16, so those run with an adult along.

Money & weather

Prices here are Canadian dollars; tap-to-pay is universal. Mid-July is peak summer — warm and dry in town, cooler and breezy in the alpine (bring a layer for Peak 2 Peak and the evening show). Long daylight, ~9:15pm sunset.

Hours that bite

Audain Art Museum is closed Tue/Wed (do it Thu Jul 16). SLCC is closed Mon. Whistler Sliding Centre self-tour runs Wed–Sun. Capilano timed windows, Come From Away, and Vallea Lumina showtimes are fixed — book the slot you want.
