Turtle Option August timing ✦

With Tortuguero

San José · Tortuguero · Arenal · Manuel Antonio
🎭 Teatro Nacional 🐢 Sea Turtles 🌋 Arenal Hot Springs 🦥 Sloths & Monkeys 🤿 Snorkeling

August is peak nesting season — the most important green turtle nesting site in the Western Hemisphere. You may watch a 300-pound turtle come ashore at night. One boat transfer (La Pavona → Tortuguero); all other legs are road shuttles.

Strengths
  • Once-in-a-lifetime night turtle tour
  • Caribbean canal wildlife (caimans, river otters, 3 monkey species)
  • Timing is perfect — peak August nesting
Tradeoffs
  • One boat-only transfer (not hard, just different)
  • Tortuguero has no ATM; bring cash
  • Slightly more logistically moving

Option A

With Monteverde

San José · Monteverde · Arenal · Manuel Antonio
🎭 Teatro Nacional 🦜 Quetzals 🌿 Cloud Forest 🌋 Arenal Hot Springs 🦥 Sloths & Monkeys 🤿 Snorkeling

Swaps the Caribbean canals for the cloud forest. Every transfer is a road shuttle — no boats, no docks, simpler logistics. Two completely different ecosystems back to back: misty mountain forest followed by volcanic lowland jungle.

Strengths
  • Simpler logistics — all road, no boats
  • Hanging bridges at canopy level (1.9mi circuit)
  • Quetzals, cloud forest night walk, Bat Jungle
  • Quaker settler history is a genuine cultural note
Tradeoffs
  • No sea turtles
  • Quetzals are harder to find in August (peak is March–June)
  • Monteverde can be cold and misty — bring layers
